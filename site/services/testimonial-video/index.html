About This Format

Testimonial Production

Testimonial videos are the most trusted form of marketing content. When a real customer explains how your product or service changed their business — in their own words, with real emotion — it resonates in a way that no brand messaging ever can.

We offer two approaches to testimonial videos. First, our animated testimonial format: we take a written review or transcript and bring it to life with custom animation, professional voiceover, and branded visuals. Second, we can guide the production of live-action testimonials remotely — providing your customers with a simple recording guide and editing the footage into a polished final video.

Testimonial videos are most effective when they follow a specific structure: the customer's situation before, the challenge they were facing, why they chose you, and the specific outcome they achieved. We help you craft that narrative for maximum impact.
