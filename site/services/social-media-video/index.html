About This Format

Social Media Production

Social media is a video-first environment. Text posts, static images, and link shares are increasingly invisible — while short-form video content dominates every major platform. If your brand isn't producing consistent, high-quality video content, you're invisible to your audience.

We create animated social media videos in 15–60 second formats for TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook. Our content is designed to stop the scroll, communicate your message in the first 3 seconds, and drive the action you want — whether that's a follow, a click, or a purchase.

We offer two delivery models. First, one-off campaign videos: a batch of 3–5 videos for a specific launch or promotion. Second, monthly content retainers: regular delivery of branded animated content to keep your channels active and your audience growing.

According to HubSpot's 2026 Social Media Trends report, short-form video has the highest ROI of any social media format for the third year running. The brands winning on social are the ones publishing consistently — and consistency is easier with animation than live production.
