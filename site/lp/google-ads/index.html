How much does an explainer video cost?

How long does it take to produce?

AI-Video is delivered in 5–7 business days. Custom 2D Animation typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on complexity and revision rounds. Premium productions vary. We always agree on a fixed delivery date before starting.

What's included in the price?

Everything you need to publish. Script writing (2D & Premium), professional voiceover, animation, background music, sound effects, and delivery in all formats (landscape 16:9, square 1:1, vertical 9:16). No add-ons, no surprises.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. If you're not satisfied with the final result, we offer a full refund — no questions asked. In 10+ years of business we've never had to use it, because we don't start production until you've approved the script and storyboard.

What formats do you deliver?

All videos are delivered in MP4 format at 1080p or higher. You'll receive landscape (16:9) for YouTube and websites, square (1:1) for Facebook and Instagram feed, and vertical (9:16) for Stories, Reels, and TikTok — all included.
