Video Marketing Statistics 2026: 50+ Facts You Need to Know

2026-03-17  ·  8 min read

Every year, Wyzowl publishes their State of Video Marketing report — the most comprehensive look at how businesses use video and what results they get. The 2026 edition surveyed over 900 marketers and consumers. Here's what they found — plus additional data from HubSpot, Cisco, and others.

Video Adoption Statistics

  • 91% of businesses use video as a marketing tool in 2026 — up from 86% in 2022. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 96% of marketers say video is an important part of their marketing strategy. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 88% of marketers say they will increase or maintain their video budget in 2026. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • Video is the #1 form of content consumed online, ahead of social media posts, news articles, and blogs. (HubSpot, 2026)

Consumer Behaviour Statistics

  • 84% of people say they've been convinced to buy a product after watching a brand's video. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 72% of customers say they prefer to learn about a product or service through video. (HubSpot, 2026)
  • Viewers retain 95% of a message delivered through video versus 10% through text. (Insivia)
  • People watch an average of 17 hours of online video per week — up from 10.5 hours in 2018. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 80% of video marketers say video has directly helped increase sales. (Wyzowl, 2026)

Explainer Video Statistics

  • 96% of people have watched an explainer video to learn more about a product or service. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 89% of people have been convinced to buy a product or service by watching an explainer video. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • Landing pages with video convert up to 80% better than those without. (Unbounce)
  • The average viewer watches 84% of an explainer video — compared to only reading 20% of a text page. (Wistia)
  • Websites with video are 53x more likely to appear on the first page of Google. (Forrester Research)

Social Media Video Statistics

  • Short-form video has the highest ROI of any social media format for the third year running. (HubSpot, 2026)
  • Video posts on LinkedIn get 5x more engagement than other content types. (LinkedIn, 2026)
  • TikTok is now the second-most used platform for product discovery, behind only Google Search. (GWI, 2026)
  • Instagram Reels get 22% more interaction than regular video posts. (Socialinsider, 2025)

ROI & Business Impact Statistics

  • 87% of marketers say video has directly increased website traffic. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 82% say video has helped increase dwell time on their website. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • 95% of video marketers plan to maintain or increase their video spend in the next 12 months. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • Video in email increases click-through rates by 200–300%. (HubSpot)
  • Including video on a landing page can increase conversion rates by up to 80%. (Unbounce)

B2B Video Statistics

  • 70% of B2B buyers watch videos throughout their purchase journey. (Google)
  • 54% of B2B decision-makers watch video content every week. (Google)
  • Explainer videos are the #1 most common type of marketing video for B2B brands. (Wyzowl, 2026)
  • B2B companies using video in their marketing grow revenue 49% faster year-over-year. (Aberdeen Group)

What This Means for Your Business

The data is clear: video isn't optional anymore. Whether you're a SaaS startup, a healthcare company, or a solo founder — video is the highest-ROI marketing investment you can make in 2026.
