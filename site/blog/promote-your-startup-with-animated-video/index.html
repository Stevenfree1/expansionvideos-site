Promote Your Startup With Animated Video

July 2, 2024

Startups face a unique marketing challenge: you have a complex idea that most people have never encountered before, a tiny marketing budget, and about 8 seconds to convince someone your product is worth their attention.

Animated explainer video was practically invented for this problem.

Why Animation Is Perfect for Startups

You can explain the unexplainable

Most startup products don't exist yet in the physical world — they're software, platforms, networks, or concepts. Animation lets you visualize anything: how data flows through your system, what the user experience feels like, or why the problem you're solving actually costs companies millions.

It scales with your growth

A well-made explainer video works at seed stage, Series A, and beyond. You can use the same video on your homepage, in pitch decks, at trade shows, and in paid ads for years.

It makes you look bigger than you are

A professionally produced animated video signals investment and credibility. For a startup competing against established players, this matters. Visitors who land on your homepage can't tell whether you have 3 employees or 300 — but they can tell whether you've invested in communicating clearly.

Using Video at Different Startup Stages

Pre-launch / MVP

Use a simple explainer video on a "coming soon" landing page to validate demand before building. A waitlist powered by a compelling video is the best market research you can do.

Launch

Your launch video is your first impression at scale. Keep it under 90 seconds, focus on the core problem and solution, and end with a clear next step (sign up, book a demo, start for free).

Growth stage

As you scale, create videos for specific segments — enterprise, SMB, different industries. The startup that has a tailored message for each audience converts significantly better than the one with a single generic pitch.
