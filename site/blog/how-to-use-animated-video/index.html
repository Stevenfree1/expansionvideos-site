How to Use Animated Video: The Complete Guide

September 18, 2024

Animated video is one of the most flexible and effective marketing tools available — but only when it's used strategically. This guide covers everything from picking the right format to measuring results.

Step 1: Choose the Right Format

Not all animated videos are the same. Here are the three main categories:

  • AI-Video ($299–$899): Fast production (5–7 days), AI-generated animation, human voiceover. Best for social media ads, quick campaigns, and volume production.
  • 2D Animation ($799–$2,499): Custom-designed characters and scenes, unlimited revisions, 3–5 week production. The standard choice for most businesses.
  • Premium/Custom (custom quote): The highest level of production quality, for brands that demand the absolute best.

Step 2: Define the Script

The script is the most important element of any explainer video. A weak script can't be saved by great animation. A strong script can make even modest animation highly effective.

The classic explainer video structure:

  1. Hook (0–5s): Start with the problem or a bold statement
  2. Problem (5–15s): Empathize with the viewer's pain point
  3. Solution (15–40s): Introduce your product/service as the answer
  4. How it works (40–60s): Explain the key steps or features
  5. Call to action (60–90s): Tell the viewer exactly what to do next

Step 3: Production

Once the script is approved, production follows four stages: voiceover recording, storyboard/design, animation, and final delivery. At ExpansionVideos, we involve you at every stage — you approve the script, choose the voice actor, review the storyboard, and give feedback on the animation before final delivery.

Step 4: Distribution

Where your video lives determines who sees it. Key channels:

  • Homepage: Above the fold, ideally with a custom thumbnail
  • YouTube: Optimized title, description, and tags for SEO
  • LinkedIn: Native video performs 5x better than link posts
  • Email campaigns: "Video" in the subject line increases opens
  • Paid social: Cut down versions for ads (15–30 seconds)

Step 5: Measure and Optimize

Track view rate (the percentage who watch more than 30 seconds), completion rate, and downstream conversion metrics (sign-ups, contact form submissions, sales). Use these to brief your next video better.
