AI Video vs Traditional Animation: Which Is Right for You?

February 10, 2026  ·  6 min read

It's hard to ignore what's happened in video production over the past couple of years. AI tools have gone from curiosity to legitimate production option — and the question we get asked constantly is: "Should I go AI or traditional animation?"

Honest answer: it depends on what you're trying to achieve. Here's a real breakdown.

What "AI Video" Actually Means

When people say "AI video," they usually mean one of a few things:

  • AI-generated scenes: Text-to-video tools that generate footage from a prompt (think Sora, Kling, Pika)
  • AI-assisted animation: Using AI to speed up traditional animation workflows — auto-in-betweening, lip sync, background generation
  • Talking head / avatar video: AI presenters like HeyGen where a digital human reads your script
  • Hybrid production: AI for some elements (backgrounds, motion) combined with human design and direction

At ExpansionVideos, our AI video offering is a hybrid approach — we use AI tools intelligently as part of a directed production process, not a "prompt and hope" workflow. See our services page for details.

The Case for AI Video

AI video has real, concrete advantages in the right context:

  • Speed: A well-produced AI video can be delivered in 1–2 weeks vs 4–6 weeks for full 2D animation
  • Cost: Significantly lower price point — making video accessible for smaller budgets
  • Iteration: Easier to update if your product changes or messaging evolves
  • Volume: Ideal for brands that need multiple videos (different languages, A/B tests, product variations)

If you're a startup testing messaging, a company that needs videos in multiple markets, or working with a limited budget, AI video is a serious option.

The Case for Traditional Animation

Traditional 2D animation still has advantages that AI genuinely can't match — at least not yet:

  • Brand consistency: Custom characters, custom colour palette, custom visual style that's uniquely yours
  • Storytelling depth: Human animators craft emotional arcs, comedic timing, and visual metaphors with intentionality
  • Control: Every frame is intentional. Nothing is random or "good enough for AI"
  • Premium feel: For enterprise sales, investor pitches, or flagship brand moments — quality signals matter
  • IP ownership: With traditional animation, every asset is created for you and owned by you

If you're pitching to enterprise clients, building a long-term brand asset, or need a video that reflects the quality of your product, traditional animation is the better investment.

A Practical Decision Framework

Here's how to think about it:

The Honest Reality

The best video is the one that gets made and actually used. A €5,000 traditional animation that sits in a Dropbox folder because launch got delayed is worth less than a €600 AI video that goes live and starts driving leads tomorrow.
