How Much Does an Explainer Video Cost in 2026?

2026-03-17  ·  7 min read

If you've been searching for "how much does an explainer video cost," you've probably found answers ranging from $500 to $50,000. The range is genuinely that wide — and understanding why will help you make a much smarter buying decision.

The cost of an explainer video depends on four main factors: style, length, quality level, and who makes it. Let's break down each tier honestly.

Explainer Video Price Tiers in 2026

DIY Tools: $0–$100/month

Tools like Animaker, Vyond, and Canva let you build animated videos yourself with drag-and-drop templates. The results look like templates — which is the problem. Template-based videos don't differentiate your brand and often communicate a lack of professionalism. Best for: internal presentations, social media experiments, very early-stage startups with no budget.

Freelancers: $200–$1,500

A solo animator on Fiverr or Upwork can produce an animated explainer for $200–$1,500 depending on their experience and your requirements. Quality varies enormously. At the low end, you get offshore template work. At the higher end ($800–$1,500), you can find talented freelancers who produce genuinely good work. Risk is high — vetting takes time and revisions can spiral.

Boutique Studios (like us): $299–$5,000

This is the sweet spot for most growing businesses. A boutique studio brings structured process, professional voiceover, custom animation, and project management — without the overhead of a large agency. At ExpansionVideos, our packages start at $299 for AI-assisted video and go up to $4,500 for premium custom 2D animation with unlimited revisions.

What you get at this tier: professional brief process, script writing, voiceover artist selection, custom animation (not templates), and unlimited revisions until you're satisfied. Most projects deliver in 3–4 weeks.

Mid-Size Agencies: $5,000–$15,000

Established video agencies with larger teams and more process overhead. You get account management, multiple creative options, and often a longer production timeline (6–10 weeks). Best for: companies with marketing teams, brand guidelines, and complex products that need multiple stakeholder reviews.

Premium / Top-Tier Studios: $15,000–$50,000+

The Wyzowls and Buck Designs of the world. Exceptional quality, bespoke animation, celebrity voiceover, custom music composition. Appropriate for: Fortune 500 campaigns, major product launches, content that will be shown to millions of people repeatedly.

What Drives the Price Up?

  • Length: Every 30 seconds adds roughly 20-30% to the cost. Keep it tight — 60 seconds is usually enough.
  • Style complexity: Character animation costs more than motion graphics. 3D costs more than 2D.
  • Voiceover: A professional studio recording costs $150–$500 extra vs. a remote artist.
  • Revisions: Agencies with revision limits charge extra per round. Look for unlimited revision policies.
  • Music: Custom-composed music adds $500–$2,000. Licensed tracks are much cheaper.
  • Translations: Each language version is roughly 40–60% of the original video cost.

What's the Right Budget for Your Business?

Here's a practical guide based on your situation:

  • Pre-revenue startup: $299–$500. Start with an AI-assisted video or a focused 60-second explainer. Get something professional on your homepage.
  • Growing startup ($1M–$10M ARR): $500–$2,500. Invest in quality 2D animation with custom characters. This video will represent your brand for 2–3 years.
  • Scale-up / SMB: $2,500–$8,000. Custom animation, professional studio voiceover, multiple formats for ads and social.
  • Enterprise: $10,000+. Premium quality, brand compliance reviews, multiple languages, regional versions.
