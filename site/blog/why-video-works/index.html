Why Video Works: The Science Behind Animated Explainer Videos

March 12, 2025

Have you ever read a long product description and immediately forgotten most of it? Now compare that to a 60-second animated video that explains the same product. Which did you remember better?

This isn't just intuition — it's neuroscience. And it explains exactly why animated explainer videos have become one of the most powerful marketing tools available today.

The Dual-Coding Theory

Psychologist Allan Paivio's dual-coding theory states that the brain processes verbal and visual information through two separate but interconnected systems. When both systems are activated simultaneously — as they are in animated video — retention increases dramatically.

Studies consistently show that people remember up to 68% of information they receive through both audio and visual channels, compared to just 10% through text alone.

Why Animation Works Even Better Than Live Video

You might wonder: if video is powerful, why animation specifically? The answer comes down to cognitive load and control.

  • No distractions: Animation removes real-world visual clutter. There are no actors to judge, no backgrounds to distract from. The viewer's full attention goes to the message.
  • Visual metaphors: Abstract concepts (like "cloud computing" or "data security") can be visualized in ways that live video can't achieve.
  • Controlled pacing: The scriptwriter controls exactly how fast information is delivered, ensuring the viewer isn't overwhelmed.
  • Brand consistency: Colors, characters, and style can be designed to match your brand perfectly — every frame reinforces your visual identity.

The Statistics That Matter

Beyond neuroscience, the marketing data backs this up:

  • Landing pages with explainer videos convert up to 80% better than those without
  • 57% of consumers watch product videos before making a purchase decision
  • Viewers who watch an explainer video are 4x more likely to buy than those who just read about it
  • Video is 40x more likely to be shared on social media than text or still images

What This Means for Your Business

If your product or service requires explanation — and most do — then animated video isn't a "nice to have." It's a core marketing asset that directly impacts your conversion rate.
