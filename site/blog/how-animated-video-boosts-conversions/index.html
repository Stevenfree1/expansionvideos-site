How Animated Explainer Videos Boost Conversions by Up to 80%

February 20, 2025

The headline number gets thrown around a lot: "explainer videos boost conversions by 80%." But where does that number come from, and more importantly — can you actually achieve results like that?

Let's look at the data, the psychology, and what it takes to make a video that actually moves the needle.

The Research Behind the Numbers

The 80% figure comes from multiple independent studies analyzing the performance of landing pages with and without explainer videos. Here's a sample of what the data shows:

  • Wyzowl's Video Marketing Statistics report found that 87% of marketers say video has directly increased sales
  • Unbounce's Landing Page Analyzer found that pages with video see an average 65-80% improvement in conversions
  • Forrester Research found that one minute of video is worth 1.8 million words in terms of information conveyed

Why Explainer Videos Convert

Understanding why helps you create better videos. There are three core mechanisms:

1. They reduce cognitive load

When a visitor arrives on your website, they're making fast decisions. Text requires effort — the brain has to parse, interpret, and store information. Video does this work for the viewer, lowering the barrier between arriving and understanding.

2. They build trust faster

A well-crafted explainer video signals investment in quality. It tells visitors: "This company cares enough to explain themselves properly." That trust is worth more than any headline you can write.

3. They answer objections proactively

A good script anticipates the viewer's questions and concerns, addressing them in a natural narrative flow. By the time the video ends, the visitor has fewer objections left — and is more ready to act.

How to Maximize Your Video's Conversion Impact

Not all videos are created equal. Here's what separates high-converting explainer videos from forgettable ones:

  • Strong opening: The first 5 seconds are critical. Lead with the problem or the hook — not your company name.
  • Single clear message: Focus on one core benefit or use case. Trying to say everything means communicating nothing.
  • Clear call to action: End with a specific, simple action you want the viewer to take.
  • Right length: 60–90 seconds is the sweet spot for most explainer videos. Long enough to tell the story, short enough to keep attention.
