Video Marketing ROI: How to Measure What Matters

January 20, 2026  ·  6 min read

Every marketing investment eventually faces the same question: is it working? Video is no different — but it's uniquely tricky to measure because its effects ripple through your entire funnel. A video watched today might drive a conversion six months from now.

Here's how to think about video marketing ROI properly.

The Problem with "Vanity Metrics"

The most common mistake in measuring video ROI is focusing on views. Views feel satisfying — a video with 50,000 views seems more successful than one with 5,000. But views alone tell you almost nothing about business impact.

A video with 5,000 views that's placed on a high-converting landing page and watched by decision-makers in your target market is worth far more than a viral video watched by people who will never buy from you.

Stop optimising for views. Start optimising for outcomes.

The Metrics That Actually Matter

1. Watch-through Rate

What percentage of viewers watch to the end? A 70%+ completion rate on a 90-second video is excellent. Below 40% and you likely have a script or pacing problem. This metric tells you whether the video is actually communicating your message.

2. Conversion Rate (with and without video)

The cleanest ROI measurement: run an A/B test with your landing page — one version with video, one without. The conversion rate difference is direct proof of impact. Most clients see 20–80% improvement. This is the number that justifies the investment.

3. Time on Page

Pages with video have dramatically higher average session duration. If your page without video averages 45 seconds and with video it averages 2.5 minutes, that's meaningful — it signals engagement and comprehension.

4. Assisted Conversions

In Google Analytics, check the "assisted conversions" report. Video views (tracked as events) that appear in the path to a conversion — even if video wasn't the last touch — count as assisted conversions. This captures video's influence even when it's not the direct conversion trigger.

5. Sales Cycle Length

For B2B businesses especially, video can shorten the sales cycle by answering questions proactively. If prospects who watched your explainer video close faster or require fewer calls, that's measurable ROI even if it doesn't show up in a standard conversion report.

How to Calculate the Number

Here's a simple framework:

  1. Measure your baseline conversion rate on a key page (e.g., 2.5%)
  2. Add video and measure again after 30 days (e.g., 3.8%)
  3. Calculate the lift: 3.8 / 2.5 = 52% improvement
  4. Apply that to your monthly leads / revenue to quantify the value

If that page generates €10,000/month in pipeline, a 52% improvement is worth €5,200/month. A video costing €1,500 pays for itself in the first week.

Longer-Term ROI

Unlike paid ads, which stop working the moment you stop paying, a video is a permanent asset. A well-produced explainer video from three years ago is still driving conversions today for many of our clients. Factor that longevity into your ROI calculation — the denominator (cost) stays fixed while the numerator (value generated) keeps growing.

The Non-Quantifiable ROI

Some of the most valuable effects of a great video don't appear in a dashboard:

  • Prospects arrive on sales calls already educated — less time explaining basics
  • Your brand feels more credible and professional
  • Your team has a consistent, polished way to explain the product
  • Customer support volume decreases as the video answers common questions

These are real. They're just harder to put a number on.
